WHAT THIS IS

Hoot is a finished instrument, not a synth you have to design before you can play. Seven owls sit on a hexagonal play-field. Tap one to play it. Each owl is a preset slot.

START HERE

PITCH and RATIO are where the most interesting sounds live. Pitch sets the fundamental frequency (20–200 Hz, weighted low). Ratio sets the FM character, from clean integer ratios (warm) to inharmonic territory (bell-like, metallic, unsettling).

Twist those first. The other knobs add detail.

PITCH CAST

Tap PITCH CAST to broadcast the selected owl's character across all 7 slots as a major scale. The cast retunes each slot so the whole row sounds like one instrument played across an octave — not seven copies of the same patch transposed crudely. This is the move that makes Hoot playable as a melodic instrument.

PLAY GESTURES

Tap an owl to play it. Press and hold to sustain — the owl keeps ringing as long as your finger is down, then releases naturally when you lift.

VOICE MODES

POLY — owls overlap freely. MONO — only one rings at a time. LEGATO — pitch glides between notes. Glide time is on the slider.

PRESETS

The OWLERY tab holds saved owls and parliaments. Tap a row to load. Long-press to delete. The dropdowns above the hex let you load owls directly into specific slots.
